Graycie automatically switches Android's grayscale color correction according
to the app currently in the foreground.

Choose one of two policies:

• Only selected apps: selected apps are grayscale; everything else stays in color.
• Except selected apps: selected apps stay in color; everything else is grayscale.

Graycie also includes optional auto-snooze support for banking and other
security-sensitive apps that refuse to run while an accessibility service is
enabled. “Open safely” disables Graycie's accessibility service before opening
one of these apps. You remain in control of when the service resumes.

Important setup and privacy details:

• Graycie uses an accessibility service solely to identify the foreground app.
  It does not read screen text, inspect window content, perform gestures, or
  collect what you type.
• Graycie reads the installed-app catalog so you can select apps for grayscale
  and auto-snooze policies.
• A one-time ADB command must grant WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS. This lets Graycie
  control Android's color-correction setting and restore its own accessibility
  service after a snooze. The permission cannot be granted from within the app.
• Foreground-app detection, policy decisions, settings, and app lists remain
  entirely on your device. Graycie has no network permission, analytics,
  account, ads, cloud service, or telemetry.
• An ongoing local notification is shown while accessibility is snoozed so you
  can return to Graycie and resume it.

Graycie always keeps its own interface in color. Home and launcher-hosted
Overview remain grayscale, and the current Home app cannot be selected.
